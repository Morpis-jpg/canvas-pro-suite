Welcome to gCalc

One app for graphing, calculations, symbolic algebra and physics — pick a mode in the toolbar up top.

1
Graphing

Plot y = x^2, implicit curves like x^2 + y^2 = 9, and inequalities. Add tables and fit a curve with one click.

2
Scientific

A full calculator: trig in RAD or DEG, log/ln, powers & roots, π and e constants, plus nCr/nPr, Γ, factorial and more.

3
CAS

Symbolic algebra — simplify, expand, derivative, rationalize, and solving equations like x^2 - 4 = 0 exactly.

4
AP Physics

13 reference topics plus an equation solver: type v = u + a*t, pick which variable to find, fill in the rest and hit Solve.

5
AP Chem

13 chemistry topics with the same solver: type P*V = n*R*T or pH = -log10(H) and solve for any variable.

6
AP Bio

Population growth, Hardy–Weinberg, water potential, chi-square and more: type N = N0*e^(r*t) and solve for any variable.

7
AP Calc

Derivative rules, related rates, Euler's method, series and more: type L = fa + m*(x - a) and solve for any variable.

8
AP Stats

z-scores, normal CDF, confidence intervals, binomial probabilities and more: type z = (x - mu)/sigma and solve for any variable.

9
SAT & ACT

Math reference for both exams — slope, percents, quadratics, geometry, trig: type m = (y2-y1)/(x2-x1) and solve for any variable.

Tip: the ⚙ gear in the Graphing corner toggles the grid, numeric labels and degrees; results can be replotted or re-solved.
